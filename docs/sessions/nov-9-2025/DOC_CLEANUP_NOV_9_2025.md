## 🎓 Lessons Learned

### What Worked Well
1. **Archive Pattern**: Using `docs/archive/previous-sprints/` maintains history
2. **Version Updates**: Incrementing version in `00_START_HERE.md` shows progress
3. **⚡ Markers**: NEW markers help users find latest features
4. **Multiple Entry Points**: Both `00_START_HERE.md` and `00_UNIFICATION_INDEX.md` work well

### Future Recommendations
1. **Regular Archiving**: Archive sprint reports monthly or after major milestones
2. **Index Updates**: Update main indexes immediately after creating new doc suites
3. **File Naming**: Consider `docs/sessions/YYYY-MM-DD/` for date-based organization
4. **Automated Cleanup**: Could script detection of "COMPLETE" and "SUCCESS" files for archiving

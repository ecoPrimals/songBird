## Maintenance Guidelines

### When to Archive
1. **End of each session**: Move session reports to parent archive
2. **Monthly**: Archive previous month's status documents
3. **After milestones**: Archive completed milestone reports
4. **Version releases**: Archive pre-release documentation

### What to Keep in Root
- **Current status** (STATUS_DEC_1_2025.md)
- **Essential docs** (README, START_HERE, CONTRIBUTING)
- **Active session reports** (current month only)
- **Configuration guides** (always relevant)

### What to Archive
- **Superseded status reports** (older than current)
- **Completed session reports** (after session ends)
- **Historical analysis** (already referenced in current docs)
- **Backup/temporary files** (delete, don't archive)

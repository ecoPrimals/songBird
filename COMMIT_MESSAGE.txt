- ✅ Added `port` field to discovery messages
- ✅ Peers can construct full HTTPS endpoints automatically
- ✅ Backward compatible (supports v2.0 and v2.1)
- ✅ Enhanced peer logging with connection info

### 3. IPv4/IPv6 Smart Handling
- ✅ Default to IPv4 (0.0.0.0) for maximum compatibility
- ✅ Works on IPv4-only and dual-stack networks
- ✅ Can override with SONGBIRD_BIND_ADDRESS="[::]"
- ✅ Fixed IPv6 binding issues

### 4. Multi-Federation Architecture
- ✅ `MultiFederationState` - Manage multiple federations simultaneously
- ✅ `FederationContext` - Per-federation policies and isolation
- ✅ `DiscoveryRouter` - Smart peer routing (IP, capability, time-based)
- ✅ `AutoJoinPolicy` - Capability-based filtering
- ✅ `IpNetwork` - Subnet matching for routing
- ✅ `TrustPolicy` - Per-federation trust requirements
- ✅ `ResourceQuota` - Per-federation resource limits
- ✅ `DataPolicy` - Per-federation data isolation

### 5. Discovery → Federation Bridge
- ✅ Integrated into orchestrator startup
- ✅ Polls discovered peers every 10 seconds
- ✅ Logs peer discovery with capabilities
- ✅ Foundation for automatic federation join
